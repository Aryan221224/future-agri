AT EACH STATION (6 captures):
  1. panel_before   Calibrated reference panel filling >=30% of frame, same distance every time, camera not shadowing it
  2. nadir_canopy   Camera looking straight down at canopy from fixed height (1.5-2.0 m), monopod or tripod arm, same height every visit
  3. oblique_row    45 deg oblique along the crop row, for canopy structure and to see leaf undersides where many diseases start
  4. closeup_leaf   Macro of 3-5 representative leaves at ~0.3 m, for lesion-scale detail the drone can never resolve
  5. wide_context   Wide shot showing the station marker and surroundings, so a reviewer can tell later whether the sample was representative
  6. panel_after    Reference panel again -- brackets the station's illumination and catches a cloud passing mid-capture
